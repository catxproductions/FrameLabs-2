Interpretation: This is a qualitative, reduced-order teaching model. Material capacities, contact friction, debris fragmentation, impact resistance, kinetic-energy transfer, impulse, fracture thresholds, deformation and shockwave propagation use illustrative units, not calibrated real-world properties or engineering calculations. The model now approximates pre-failure beam sag, catenary tension, angle-dependent joint shear, plastic joint yielding, short frictional anchoring, and damped separation, but does not validate actual building safety.
Tips: Resize the grid up to 40 bays × 24 levels. Click and drag to paint continuous lines of columns, beams, slabs, diagonal braces, or X-bracing. Build continuous columns from the foundation, connect them with beams, add slabs, then select Validate. Apply loads to individual bays and use the heatmap to inspect weak points. Explosion applies a virtual radial impulse with distance-based damage; power is adjustable. Ignite starts a fictional fire on flammable composites or triggers explosive composites. Fire spreads through connected flammable members and burns them down over time. Demolish removes a member immediately; physics failures create movable debris. Save JSON exports the design and member damage state (not a live replay). Keyboard shortcuts: B build · D demolish · L load · P push · E explosion · I ignite · T gravity tether · V validate · Space simulate/pause · H hide UI · O toggle annotations. Members fracture into two separate half-length debris pieces when they fail.

⚠ CPU / performance warning

Large simulations can be demanding

FrameLab calculates structural connections, stress, impacts, debris, fire, and explosions locally in your browser. Large example buildings—especially Large Cinema—can use significant CPU and may make slower devices lag or become warm.

  • Start with a smaller frame if performance drops.
  • Pause the simulation while editing or inspecting a design.
  • Repeated explosions, active fire, and dense debris can increase CPU use.
  • Use Reset physics to clear accumulated motion and debris.

This warning appears each time you open or reload FrameLab.
